# Mobile verification: RA business and security audit

Reviewed on 9 November 2026 against the implementation, automated business scenarios, the [6 February 2026 SEBI Master Circular for Research Analysts](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/master-circulars/feb-2026/master-circular-for-research-analysts_99571.html), and the [23 October 2026 communication-record circular, effective 8 November 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/oct-2026/1792753022042.pdf). This is a feature-specific engineering/business audit, not an annual statutory compliance audit or certification of the wider onboarding product.

## Business disposition

The design is suitable for a controlled trial once the vendor account, approved DLT template and deployment settings are supplied. Production activation remains subject to real-provider evidence, operational retention controls and the existing wider blockers in [the RA production review](SEBI_RA_REVIEW.md). Software OTP completion is a contact-possession check, not KRA validation, PAN ownership verification, an agreement signature or consent to paid research.

## Findings corrected during implementation

1. **RA-owned SMS recovery would cross an account trust boundary.** Provider-console access could expose reset OTPs. RA template/credential control now applies only to their KYC verification; shared-account password recovery uses independent platform credentials and a platform-approved template. An RA cannot change that sender/template through website configuration. Recovery is refused if the platform username appears in any saved RA configuration.
2. **A membership phone timestamp alone could become stale.** Approval and checkout now compare the canonical current phone with the phone in the relevant KYC/agreement evidence. An unrelated edit clears the stamp. Draft changes and owner-assisted edits cannot preserve proof for a different number.
3. **Changing a previously reviewed contact could strand or improperly preserve KYC.** Re-verifying the same contact leaves approval intact. Changing an unpaid client's submitted contact moves KYC to changes, advances its revision and appends encrypted history. A paid contact replacement requires the separate controlled process described above.
4. **Wrong-code failures must persist across requests.** Attempt updates commit before returning an error. Resend does not reset guesses, and account/mobile budgets span new flows and RAs. Concurrent proof consumption permits only one success. Recovery challenges are bound to the credential version at issuance; a later email reset or other password change invalidates old SMS codes.
5. **Provider timeouts could cause duplicate billing or unusable late codes.** Reservations commit before sending; there is no automatic resend. An uncertain request retains the challenge so the investor can use a code that arrives late. Quotas include uncertain sends.
6. **Audit access must not reveal active recovery secrets.** Full evidence is encrypted and kept outside public website data. RA exports contain only their mobile-verification communications with OTPs redacted; platform recovery evidence remains outside the RA interface. General logs/errors contain no credentials, phone numbers or OTPs.

## Conditions before real use

- Supply real RA-approved sender/template credentials through the private UI. For recovery, separately provision a platform-owned SMS Horizon account and approved template in `SMS_RECOVERY_SETTINGS_JSON`. Provider-console isolation requires operational review; a username comparison alone cannot establish who has account access.
- Apply the additive migration explicitly and preserve the existing retained data key. Migration startup checks fail closed; the feature does not silently migrate an application database or mark old phones verified.
- Keep trial mode restricted to the approved actual recipient. Do not use the test number to represent another investor. Automated tests use fake SMS and email boundaries, not live trial credits.
- Verify the provider's account/entity/header/template linkage, real delivery, Unicode/segment billing, balance, credential/IP restrictions and error behavior. The current release records acceptance/uncertainty and does not poll delivery receipts. Delivery is never the authority for verification.
- Review each RA’s privacy notice and processor arrangements for SMS phone-number processing. The verification request must not be treated as consent to research terms or marketing.
- Establish five-year communication retention and extended preservation for disputes/SEBI directions. The October 2026 circular expressly includes SMS interactions and records from prospective-client interactions. The database retains evidence but does not provide independent immutable storage, legal-hold enforcement or tested disaster recovery by itself.
- Configure SMTP, Sentry alert routing and queue monitoring, and test a real security notification. Inspect/requeue exhausted `sms_security_notices` only after resolving the cause; at-least-once delivery may duplicate a notice. A crashed final attempt can require operator discovery from queue state even without a failure callback.
- Before allowing paid-client number replacement, implement or document a separately controlled review/correction procedure that preserves signed history, verifies the new number and does not silently change global recovery. Current self-service denial is intentional; the RA's ordinary KYC-edit form cannot bypass it.
- Keep the existing production signing gate closed until the broader KYC, consent method, terms/MITC, refunds, invoice and regulated-retention findings in the production review are resolved. Adding mobile OTP does not resolve those findings.

## Password-recovery budget follow-up

SMS recovery is now limited to three sends per investor per rolling 24 hours, and email recovery to five reset-link send attempts per global identity. Both channels have a 60-second account-wide cooldown. Email remains available before SMS is consumed; quota exhaustion never triggers unsolicited email or blocks password login. Native auth reset-mail callbacks reserve email quota before SMTP, so tenant/platform/direct API calls and provider failures cannot bypass it. The additional migration preserves the original SMS migration for safe upgrades. See [the full policy](MOBILE_VERIFICATION.md#password-recovery-budgets).

## Private settings and template-library follow-up

The owner configuration now separates SMS, Telegram and payment settings from public Branding and publication. The SMS library supports up to 100 entries, with Login wired to the existing post-password-login mobile/KYC check. The protected Reset/Forgot Password starter is clearly a reference copy: RA-controlled DLT text or vendor-console access still cannot control the global password. Custom entries have no automatic sending behavior. Existing single-template configurations preserve their active verification wording on upgrade.

Private integration credentials are encrypted, independently versioned and omitted from read responses. Current owner/session/tenant authorization is rechecked at save time. Cashfree retains the existing production approval gate, and merchant editing locks once Cashfree checkout/payment evidence exists (including retained cancelled sessions), protecting the continuity of the RA's payment evidence and collection account. Telegram saves channel/bot details only; it cannot be represented as delivery of paid research or proof that access is restricted to eligible subscribers. Its activation requires a separate reviewed delivery/access implementation.

The business assessment remains suitable for a controlled trial, subject to the conditions above. OTP possession does not replace KYC review, investor agreements, communication retention or regulated service eligibility. No new production compliance approval is implied by a configured badge.
